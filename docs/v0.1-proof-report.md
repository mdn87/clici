Date: October 25, 2026. Runtime source reviewed and tested: `2c3c687de0f2984cdce274ad08d88414801b6baa`.

## Outcome and release recommendation

The local Windows proof is recorded: the [functional ledger](v0.1-proof-ledger.md)
contains eighteen passing cases and one historical observation (M07), with no
case left without a result. Two product defects found during the run were fixed:
contended reads that appeared empty, and ownerless writes incorrectly attributed
to a foreground terminal. The original M07 was not rerun after its fix; separate
post-fix verification and current unit tests support the corrected policy.

Recommend preparing the next unsigned release for continued local evaluation,
with the qualification limits below stated in its notes. Do not describe it as
a complete current-build cross-application certification. At initial closeout,
the published release was v0.1.0 and only an installer dry run had been performed.
The subsequent release record below supersedes that publication status.

Turn tracking and keyboard jumping are [retired](prompt-navigation-retirement.md).
Their experiments are excluded from the tray installer and release support claims.

## Decisions

### DEC-01 — Clipboard ownership replaces foreground fallback (TG-01)

- Trigger: M06 Notepad exit-flush and M07 hidden writer produced false positives.
- Decision: approve only a known allowed clipboard owner; skip missing owners.
- Rejected alternative: infer origin from whichever terminal is foreground.
- Effect: brokers and ownerless terminal copies can be false negatives. The
  explicit join-lines hotkey retains its documented source-gate exception.
- Evidence: separate October 17 verification and owner-policy regression tests;
  the original M07 was not rerun.

### DEC-02 — Preserve source privacy without forcing inclusion

- Evidence: privacy DWORD tests, M14, and the superseding plain-text policy.
- Decision: preserve explicit source history/cloud values, add none when absent,
  and skip monitor-excluded or unreadable privacy metadata.
- Limit: the August report's forced-inclusion experiment is historical and does
  not describe current behavior. Third-party managers may record duplicate items.

### DEC-03 — Bound RDP and clipboard-manager support to recorded environments

- Evidence: M15 and the August coexistence report.
- Decision: keep ordinary source approval rules across redirection; do not add
  `mstsc` or `rdpclip` to defaults to make redirected copies normalize.
- Limit: enabled, functioning clipboard redirection is a prerequisite. Other
  clients, servers and clipboard managers remain unqualified.

### DEC-04 — Skip rich and non-text items (CF-01)

- Evidence: M19, native-format classification and coordinator tests.
- Decision: leave the entire item intact. Do not place normalized plain text
  beside unchanged HTML/RTF/CSV. Consistent rich-format rewriting is deferred.
- Optional image export writes PNG files without replacing clipboard contents;
  it does not change this normalization policy.

### DEC-05 — Retain the existing terminal defaults

- Evidence: M02–M04 and `ConfigurationAndProcessTests`.
- Decision: retain `WindowsTerminal`, `pwsh`, `powershell`, `cmd`, `conhost`,
  `claude`, and `codex`; exclusions win. M04 proves hosted Terminal behavior,
  not direct ownership by every named CLI across all launch modes.
- Limit: VS Code/Cursor share editor and terminal processes. Focused-control
  detection and a broader source-fingerprint matrix remain deferred.

## Qualification limits before broader distribution

- M07's exact background-writer/focus-change sequence has not been rerun on the
  candidate installer. Its policy fix has separate runtime and unit evidence.
- The nineteen cases and installer lifecycle were recorded against multiple
  earlier builds. This batch does not install/uninstall the candidate or replace
  Matt's running clipboard utility.
- Image export has automated coverage for configuration, privacy skips, PNG
  writing and history handling. A current end-to-end Windows screenshot-to-WSL
  observation is not established by this proof ledger.
- The installer is unsigned; SmartScreen warnings and checksum verification
  must be disclosed. Cloud clipboard behavior and arbitrary third-party
  managers are not certified.
- Two-line wrapped copies lack enough right-edge evidence to exclude every
  mid-token seam. The README records that existing limitation.

These limits permit a clearly labelled local evaluation candidate, not an
unqualified claim of broad support.
